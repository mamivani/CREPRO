# CREPRO Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: November 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

CREPRO ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the CREPRO Property Management mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and safeguarding of your information when you use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, password, phone number
- **Property Information**: Addresses, property details, tenant information
- **Payment Information**: Invoice data, payment methods (processed securely by third-party providers)
- **Communications**: Emails, messages, and support inquiries

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Device type, OS version, app version, unique device identifier
- **Usage Data**: Features used, time spent in app, pages visited
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates (only when you explicitly enable location services)
- **Camera Photos**: Images you choose to upload for properties
- **Crash Reports**: Error logs to improve app stability

### 2.3 Camera & Gallery Access
- We request permission to access your camera for taking property photos
- We request permission to access your photo library to select existing images
- **You control**: You can enable/disable these permissions in device settings at any time

### 2.4 Location Services
- We request permission to access your location for property mapping
- **You control**: You can enable/disable location in device settings
- **Not mandatory**: Location services are optional; the app works without it

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and improve the App functionality
- Authenticate your account and prevent fraud
- Process property and invoice transactions
- Send you service updates and notifications (if you opt in)
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
- Comply with legal obligations

**We do NOT**:
- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your location for purposes other than property mapping
- Access your camera without your explicit permission

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active and 90 days after deletion
- **Transaction Data**: Retained for 7 years for tax/legal purposes
- **Photos/Documents**: Retained as long as associated property exists
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymized after 12 months

## 5. Data Security

We implement:
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted over HTTPS
- **Secure Storage**: Tokens stored in OS-level encrypted storage (Expo SecureStore)
- **No Hardcoded Credentials**: All sensitive configs use environment variables
- **Regular Audits**: Security reviews and dependency updates
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access for properties and invoices

**However**, no security is 100% guaranteed. We cannot guarantee absolute protection against all cyber threats.

## 6. Third-Party Services

We may share data with:
- **Authentication Providers**: Google, Microsoft (for OAuth login)
- **Cloud Backends**: Azure services for data storage
- **Analytics**: Anonymized usage data (no PII)
- **Payment Processors**: For invoice and payment processing
- **Law Enforcement**: If legally required

These providers have their own privacy policies.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and data (subject to legal retention)
- **Portability**: Receive your data in portable format
- **Opt-Out**: Disable notifications and analytics

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@creprotech.com

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it immediately.

## 9. International Data Transfers

If you are outside the United States, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

## 10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification.
